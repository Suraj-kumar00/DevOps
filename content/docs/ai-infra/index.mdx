This is the flagship track. It is for engineers who already run services on Kubernetes and now need to run AI workloads on it: training jobs that hold many GPUs for hours, and inference services that must answer quickly without burning money.

## Before you start

You should be comfortable with Pods, Deployments and Services, resource requests and limits, and basic autoscaling. The [DevOps track](/docs/devops) covers these.

## What you will be able to do

- Explain how Kubernetes discovers, allocates and shares GPUs, and pick a sharing strategy for a given workload.
- Choose a serving stack for traditional models and for large language models.
- Scale GPU-backed services on meaningful signals, and observe their health and utilization.
- Explain where the money goes in an AI platform, and how to reduce waste.
