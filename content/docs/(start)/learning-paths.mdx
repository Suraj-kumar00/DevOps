There is no single right order, but these paths avoid the most common trap: learning a tool before the concepts it depends on. Each step links to a track; inside a track, pages are listed in suggested reading order.

## New to DevOps

For students and engineers switching from another role.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Foundations

Linux, networking, Git, shell scripting and YAML. Everything later assumes you are comfortable here. [Open the Foundations track](/docs/foundations).

</Step>
<Step>

### DevOps

Containers, Kubernetes, CI/CD, infrastructure as code and observability, in that order. [Open the DevOps track](/docs/devops).

</Step>
<Step>

### DevSecOps

Security is part of delivery, not a later phase. Start with supply chain security and secrets management. [Open the DevSecOps track](/docs/devsecops).

</Step>
</Steps>

## From DevOps to AI infrastructure

For engineers who already run services on Kubernetes and are asked to run GPU or LLM workloads.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Check your Kubernetes basics

Scheduling, resource requests and limits, and autoscaling come up constantly in AI infrastructure. Revisit them in the [DevOps track](/docs/devops) if they feel shaky.

</Step>
<Step>

### AI Infrastructure

GPUs on Kubernetes, sharing and scheduling them, model serving and GPU observability. [Open the AI Infrastructure track](/docs/ai-infra).

</Step>
<Step>

### LLMOps, then MLOps

LLMOps covers what changes when the workload is a large language model. MLOps fills in the lifecycle around training and models. [LLMOps](/docs/llmops), then [MLOps](/docs/mlops).

</Step>
</Steps>

## From ML engineering to production

For data scientists and ML engineers who need to ship models, not only train them.

<Steps>
<Step>

### MLOps

The lifecycle from data to monitoring, and the tools that support it. [Open the MLOps track](/docs/mlops).

</Step>
<Step>

### DevOps essentials

Containers and Kubernetes basics, so you can reason about how your model actually runs. [Open the DevOps track](/docs/devops).

</Step>
<Step>

### AI Infrastructure and LLMOps

Serving, scaling and observing models in production. [AI Infrastructure](/docs/ai-infra) and [LLMOps](/docs/llmops).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Preparing for interviews

Every topic page ends with **interview scenarios** tagged by level. Read the "What and why" and "How it works" layers of the tools on the job description, then work through the scenarios for your level before opening the answers.
