This handbook is written in the open, and every contribution is reviewed and credited. You do not need to be an expert: fixing an outdated command or adding a production story from your own work is just as valuable as writing a new page.

## Ways to help

- **Report something wrong or outdated.** Every page has a "Report something wrong or outdated" link at the bottom that opens a pre-filled issue.
- **Fix a page.** Use "Edit this page on GitHub" at the bottom of any page.
- **Write a planned topic.** Each track page lists its roadmap. Pick one and open an issue first, so two people do not write the same page.
- **Add a production note or an interview scenario** from something you ran into at work.
- **Propose a new topic** that is missing from the roadmaps.

## From idea to published page

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open an issue

Describe the page, the track it belongs to and the official docs you will base it on. A maintainer confirms the scope, which saves you from rewriting later.

</Step>
<Step>

### Write the page

Follow [How pages are built](/docs/page-template): the six layers, the frontmatter, and the rules for sources and credits. Run every command and manifest you include.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open a pull request

CI builds the site, validates the frontmatter and runs the checks. Fix anything it reports, then a maintainer reviews the content.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get credited

Once merged, the page goes live on the next deploy. Your commits and pull request are your public credit.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Standards every page is held to

- Facts come from the official documentation and are linked in `sources`.
- Examples were actually run, on the version stated in `toolVersion`.
- Other people's work is linked and credited by name, never copied.
- Diagrams are drawn for this site with Mermaid.
- The page follows the [six-layer template](/docs/page-template).

## Setting up the project

To preview your changes locally or work on the site itself, follow the setup steps in [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/Suraj-kumar00/DevOps/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) in the repository.
